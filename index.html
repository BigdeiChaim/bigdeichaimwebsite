About The Program

Bigdei Chaim is a charitable program of Congregation Keren Chaim Inc., a registered nonprofit organization. The Subsidized Clothing Initiative helps kollel members in Brooklyn, Deal and Lakewood prepare for the holidays with quality clothing at a fraction of the cost.

The PackageTwo suits and three white dress shirts for a flat fee of $100.

Members receive a personal ordering card from their kollel ambassador. The card gives the details for a sizing event, where members try on suits in their size, choose a style, and place their order. Pickup is held at a later event before the holiday, and members are notified when it is planned.

This program is dedicated in memory of Vico Mizrahi, and is made possible by his family’s generosity.
